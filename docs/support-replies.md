# m.o support replies

Ready-made answers for Supportdesk_m.o@proton.me. Copy one, change the parts in [brackets], read it once, and tap Send.
Many people will find their answer on the Help page (m.o/help) first. For a quick question, you can also reply with that link.
Keep replies short and kind. Never promise something m.o doesn't do (it doesn't send texts, take payments, book places, or sync with Apple Invites).

The Privacy page promises an answer within 30 days. Check the inbox once a week.

---

## 1. "Please delete my data" or "Delete my profile"

**First:** don't delete anything yet. You need to know which profile and plans they mean.

> Hi [name],
>
> Thanks for writing. I can delete your m.o information.
>
> So I remove the right things, please reply with:
> 1. The name on your m.o profile.
> 2. The link to one plan you were part of (if you have one).
>
> If you hosted a plan, you can delete it yourself right away: open the plan, tap Edit plan details, then Delete this plan. That removes its guests, answers, items, votes and photos.
>
> I'll confirm by email once it's done.
>
> [Your first name], m.o

**Then:** start a Claude Code session, paste their reply, and say "delete this person's m.o data". I'll find it and remove it with you, step by step. When it's done, send:

> Hi [name], done. Your m.o information has been deleted. Thanks for using m.o.

## 2. "I lost my plan" or "I can't edit my plan on my new phone"

> Hi [name],
>
> m.o remembers you by the browser you made the plan in. To open it as the host on another phone:
>
> 1. On the phone or browser where you made the plan, open the plan.
> 2. Tap More tools, then Get my host link.
> 3. Send that link to yourself and open it on the new phone. Tap Open as host.
>
> Keep that link private. Anyone who has it can manage your plan.
>
> If you no longer have the first phone, reply with the plan's link and roughly when you made it, and I'll see what I can do.
>
> [Your first name], m.o

---

## 3. "My invite link doesn't work" (from a guest)

> Hi [name],
>
> Personal invite links can expire, or the host may have made a new one. Please ask your host to send you a new link from the plan. In m.o, the host taps People, then Send invites, then your name.
>
> If the host can't fix it, reply with the name of the plan and I'll take a look.
>
> [Your first name], m.o

---

## 4. "I can't see the address"

> Hi [name],
>
> The address only shows to people the host invited by name, once they say they're coming or still checking. If you joined from the group link, the host needs to OK it first.
>
> Please ask your host to open the plan, tap People, and tap Approve arrival access next to your name.
>
> [Your first name], m.o

## 7. "Can m.o text my guests for me?"

> Hi [name],
>
> m.o doesn't send texts itself. When you tap Send invites, it opens your phone's Messages app with the message filled in, and you tap Send. That way your guests' phone numbers stay on your phone.
>
> [Your first name], m.o
